How to print

  1. Print on white construction paper.
  2. Set the printer to 100% / actual size — do not fit to page.
  3. Use the exact-size PDF, or the A4 sheet and cut on the crop marks.
  4. Write a letter on the left, your address on the right. The card returns in a few months.
