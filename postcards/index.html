Postcards to Future Me

Four quiet backs at 10.7 × 13.8 cm, made for white construction paper. Write a hope or a well wish to the person you are becoming. They will be mailed back to you in a few months. All four fit on one 21.4 × 27.6 cm sheet — cut in half both ways.

How to print

  1. Print on white construction paper.
  2. Set the printer to 100% / actual size — do not fit to page.
  3. Use the 21.4 × 27.6 cm 4-up PDF. Cut in half both ways to get four cards.
  4. Write a letter on the left, your address on the right. The card returns in a few months.
