# ZenDenZen

Slow, realistic views of **mountains**, **spa**, **garden**, and **meadow**, with relaxing spa-style audio playing alongside the picture.

Live site: [https://pawsp7.github.io/ZenDenZen/](https://pawsp7.github.io/ZenDenZen/)

## How it feels

- Photoreal stills that drift very slowly, in bright, happy color
- A spa soundtrack (Mixkit) plus a live spa bed: warm pad, singing bowls, and scene air
- One click to enter, then the den keeps picture and sound together
- Scene keys `1`–`4`, space to pause, `M` to mute, `F` for fullscreen
