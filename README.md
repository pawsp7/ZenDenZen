# ZenDenZen

Locked-camera views of **mountains**, **spa**, **garden**, and **meadow**. The picture stays framed like a nature film: water, mist, leaves, rain, and fireflies keep moving, with relaxing spa-style audio beside it.

Live site: [https://pawsp7.github.io/ZenDenZen/](https://pawsp7.github.io/ZenDenZen/)

## How it feels

- Locked-camera plates, three atmospheres for each setting
- Living motion in the photograph: drifting fog and clouds, rippling water, swaying leaves, rain, steam, fireflies
- A spa soundtrack (Mixkit) plus a live spa bed: warm pad, singing bowls, and scene air
- One click to enter, then the den keeps picture and sound together
- Scene keys `1`–`4`, atmosphere `Q` `W` `E` or `[` `]`, space to pause, `M` to mute, `F` for fullscreen

## Atmospheres

- Mountains: Valley mist, Mirror lake, Star field
- Spa: Stone bath, Infinity light, Rain glass
- Garden: Rose walk, Maple pond, Lantern hour
- Meadow: Dawn fog, Lavender noon, Firefly dusk
